Retail sentiment surrounding Apple has surged to Extreme Greed, fueled by a confluence of positive catalysts and amplified by elevated social media volume. The dominant narrative centers on the resounding success of the iPhone 17, exceeding initial sales projections by 14% and sparking widespread optimism regarding Apple’s continued dominance in the consumer electronics market. This momentum is further bolstered by the unveiling of Apple Intelligence, which investors perceive as a strategically sound approach to AI integration, fostering a Fear Of Missing Out (FOMO) on the anticipated growth potential within Apple’s ecosystem. While institutional options activity reveals a mixed sentiment—some traders expressing frustration with Apple’s relative underperformance compared to peers like Nvidia—the overall buzz remains overwhelmingly bullish, propelled by analyst price target upgrades reaching $350. However, the sheer volume of enthusiasm warrants caution; such concentrated positivity often precedes periods of consolidation or correction, suggesting a potential peak in sentiment and a possible increase in volatility moving forward.
