Retail sentiment has surged to Extreme Greed, fueled by an elevated volume of conversation surrounding Apple’s recent performance and future prospects. The dominant narrative centers on the exceptional sales of the iPhone 17, exceeding prior models by 14%, coupled with the promising rollout of Apple Intelligence, which investors believe will reinforce ecosystem loyalty and spur long-term growth. This positive momentum is further amplified by a wave of analyst price target upgrades, notably from Wedbush and Citi, instilling considerable confidence in the stock. Discussions around Apple’s impending $4 trillion market capitalization add to the overall excitement, creating a palpable Fear Of Missing Out among retail investors. However, while the broader market exhibits unwavering optimism, technical analysis reveals the stock is underperforming relative to its peers, suggesting that this high volume could signal a potential near-term correction or consolidation as traders reassess valuation levels.
