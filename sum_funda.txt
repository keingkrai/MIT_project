The stock is currently overvalued, reflecting substantial growth expectations already embedded within its price. Despite this, Apple demonstrates exceptional profitability, boasting a net profit margin of nearly 27% and generating over $112 billion in net income on $416 billion in revenue, underpinned by a dominant market position and robust recurring revenue from its services segment. The company’s ability to generate nearly $100 billion in free cash flow annually provides considerable financial flexibility, yet this strength is tempered by a concerning debt-to-equity ratio of 3.87, indicating significant financial leverage and heightened sensitivity to interest rate fluctuations. While revenue growth has decelerated to just 3% year-over-year, the elevated valuation and substantial debt levels present notable risks that warrant careful consideration.
