The Neutral view suggests a measured approach amidst conflicting signals regarding current market conditions. While a strong, long-term uptrend supported by robust fundamentals remains evident, short-term overbought conditions and technical exhaustion warrant caution. A purely bullish stance, advocating full conviction, risks overlooking these vulnerabilities and potentially transforming a positive trend into a speculative gamble. Conversely, an overly conservative strategy demanding immediate position reductions could lead to a significant opportunity cost by prematurely abandoning ongoing momentum. Recognizing this risk-reward tension, a prudent compromise involves respecting the established trend while avoiding aggressive chasing of price action. For new capital allocation, a staged entry strategy is recommended—initially establishing a partial position followed by further investment on pullbacks towards key support levels like the 10 EMA or 50 SMA, thus balancing participation with disciplined risk management.
