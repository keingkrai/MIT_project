News sentiment is cautiously neutral regarding Apple, reflecting a complex interplay of challenges and opportunities. UBS recently reiterated a neutral stance on the company, citing decelerating App Store growth and demanding year-end comparisons that limit near-term gains. Regulatory headwinds further complicate the picture, as CEO Tim Cook actively urges lawmakers to reconsider the App Store Accountability Act, which threatens Apple’s services revenue model and margins. Despite these concerns, robust iPhone demand in China, fueled by aggressive logistics expansions including free three-hour deliveries, provides a significant counterbalance. Moreover, a strategic partnership with MP Materials, bolstered by Morgan Stanley’s upgrade, reinforces Apple’s supply chain resilience for crucial rare earth elements. Anticipation surrounding the upcoming foldable iPhone also contributes positively, suggesting potential for premium device sales and a refreshed upgrade cycle. However, rising memory prices, driven by AI-related demand, pose a risk of compressing margins. Expect continued volatility as the market weighs these factors and awaits clarity on regulatory outcomes.
