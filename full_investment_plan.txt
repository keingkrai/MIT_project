Section 2 Winning Argument  
The Bull analyst presented the more compelling case by grounding their argument in verifiable financial metrics and forward-looking catalysts. While valid concerns were raised about regulatory pressure and margin compression, they were not substantiated with current financial performance metrics or historical precedent showing material deterioration in Apple’s fundamentals.
